# Connect Lab

A private playground for learning how networks connect.

Connect Lab is a small simulator that runs entirely in your browser. You place cables between a laptop, a phone, a router, and a server, then send a message and watch it travel. Three short missions guide you from two devices talking directly, to using a router, to reaching a server on the Internet.

## What you will learn

- What an IP address is and why devices in the same home share the first numbers
- What a hop is: one jump of a message from one device to the next
- Why a router is needed to pass messages along and to reach the Internet

## How to use it

Open the page, tap two devices to add or cut a cable, choose who sends to whom, and press Send message.

## Safe by design

Everything happens inside the page. Connect Lab never touches your real network or any real device, so you can experiment freely.
